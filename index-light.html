James Singian is a San Francisco-based revenue leader, community architect, and career coach operating at the intersection of media, technology, and human connection. He has spent his career turning relationships into results — closing enterprise deals at the Wall Street Journal, Axios, and TechnologyAdvice while building the kind of trust that compounds long after the contract is signed.

He serves as VP of Content & Events on the executive board of sfBIG, the Bay Area's leading advertising community, and is a founding member and growth advisor at Continusum — a Bay Area collective connecting early-stage founders, creatives, and investors to move arts and culture forward through IRL experiences.

Off the sales floor, James coaches professionals who are ready to stop following the linear path and start building something that actually fits — with frameworks, community, and a clear plan to move.
